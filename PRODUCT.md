# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Broad Egyptian audience in Cairo/Giza seeking quality Syrian cuisine. Mobile-first, Arabic-native, WhatsApp-native. They discover the restaurant through social media and word-of-mouth, then use the site to browse the menu and place takeout/delivery orders.

## Product Purpose

Reda El-Sory is a Syrian restaurant in Haram, Giza. The website lets customers browse the menu by category, view deals, add items to cart, and place orders via WhatsApp. The admin panel manages menu items, deals, images, and prices. Success is converting a menu browse into a WhatsApp order with minimal friction.

## Brand Personality

Premium, warm, authentic. The brand carries the confidence of generational Syrian cooking — it doesn't need to shout. Rich tones (deep reds, gold) against warm dark backgrounds express hospitality without being opulent. The feel is a well-appointed family restaurant, not a fast-food counter.

## Anti-references

- Generic fast-food chains (bright red/yellow, crowded discount grids, drive-thru aesthetic)
- Food delivery app templates (Talabat, ToYou, Uber Eats clones)
- Overly minimal/"Scandinavian" stripping of cultural warmth
- Dark-mode as default scheme — the interface should be warm and inviting, not dark and moody

## Design Principles

1. **Premium warmth** — Upstairs but approachable. Gold and crimson accents against warm-toned backgrounds. Like a well-appointed restaurant interior.
2. **Cultural authenticity** — Syrian/Arabic visual heritage through patterns, warm tones, and hospitality cues. Not sanitized for global tastes.
3. **Menu-first** — The menu is the hero. Food photography and clear categorization drive the experience. Every other section supports the path to ordering.
4. **Frictionless ordering** — Browse → cart → WhatsApp in minimal taps. Cart state persists, checkout is one WhatsApp message away.
5. **Arabic-first** — RTL is the native layout, not a translation pass. Typography and spacing are designed for Arabic script's taller ascenders and wider glyphs.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Basic readability: sufficient text contrast, readable sizes, no reliance on color alone. Formal WCAG compliance deferred but patterns should not preclude it.
